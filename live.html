The right room matters

A stage on its own isn't the point. Plenty of gigs cost you a Saturday, a train fare and a set to twelve people who came for someone else.

The city

Somewhere you can actually get to, and somewhere your music has a reason to land.

The crowd

People who came for your kind of night, not a bill thrown together at random.

The promoter

Someone who fills rooms, looks after acts and wants to book you again.

The fee

Agreed upfront and on the table before you commit to anything.

So we don't drop you on a listings board and leave you to chase. We build the network, talk to the promoters and only put you forward for shows that make sense. Your job is to be ready when the call comes.

How Sahaya Live works

You tell us where you can play. We go looking. Three moves, in order.

  1. 01

    Join the roster

    Tell us about your music, your live setup and the cities you can realistically get to. Solo, DJ, acoustic or full band: if you can put on a show, we want to know what that show looks like.

  2. 02

    We work the network

    We build relationships with promoters, venues, agents and live partners across the UK. When they need an act, we know who's available. When you need a show, we know who to call. You stop spending your week emailing promoters. That part's on us.

  3. 03

    We bring you the show

    When there's a fit, you get the full picture: city, date, venue, set and fee. You decide. No mystery, no obligation. Say yes and we get it booked.
