How a push works

No playlist lottery and no spray-and-pray ad spend. Three moves, in order, all of them read off your own data.

  1. 01

    Pick the song

    Not every track is ready for money behind it. Sahaya reads save rate and listener behaviour across your catalogue daily and backs the one with real pull. Sometimes that isn't the single you'd have chosen.

  2. 02

    Catch the window

    There's a point where Spotify starts putting a track in front of new listeners. We watch for it and launch right then, so every pound lands while the algorithm is already leaning in. Not before, not after.

  3. 03

    Run the push

    We run the campaign end to end on the channels that fit your stage, and track every signal as it lands. You see where the streams, saves and playlist adds actually came from. Data-first reporting, never hype.

You keep making music. We handle the campaign and send you the numbers.

Every channel. The right one first.

An artist on 300 monthly listeners and an artist on 30,000 shouldn't be spending the same way. Sahaya reads where you are right now and works out which channels will do the most for your budget at this stage. As you grow, the mix changes with you.

  • Playlisting

    Puts your song in front of listeners who already live in your genre.

    Sahaya checkswhether your save rate can hold on to the listeners a playlist sends you.

  • Meta ads

    Targeted reach across Instagram and Facebook, sending people straight to the track.

    Sahaya checkswhether paid clicks are turning into saves and repeat listens, or just one-off plays.

  • UGC

    Creators using your sound in their own content, so the song travels through people, not ads.

    Sahaya checkswhether the track has the moment and the hook for creators to build around.

  • TikTok

    Campaigns built to get the sound moving on the platform where songs break.

    Sahaya checkswhether plays there are carrying over into Spotify listeners.

  • YouTube

    Video campaigns that put the song, the visuals and you in front of new viewers.

    Sahaya checkswhether video views are building an audience that follows you back to the music.

You don't pay for five channels because five channels exist.

Sahaya weighs your listener count, save rate, momentum and budget, then puts the money where it will work hardest for you right now. Sometimes that's one channel. Sometimes it's three working together. Every pound is tracked, and if a channel stops pulling its weight, we move the spend.
