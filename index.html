02 About

Built at the intersection
of artists, data
and technology.

Based Audio was built because independent labels and managers deserve the same decision intelligence that major label marketing teams have, without the infrastructure cost or headcount.

Most independent teams are making campaign decisions on weekly data cycles while streaming momentum moves daily. Reinforcement windows get missed. Budget gets committed to releases that have already peaked. The signals were there. Nobody was watching them in time.

Powered by Sahaya AGI, our proprietary decision intelligence platform gives label teams, distributors and managers accurate visibility into where every release is heading, across the entire roster, every day. Not a growth promise; a clearer view, so the decisions you make are better ones.
